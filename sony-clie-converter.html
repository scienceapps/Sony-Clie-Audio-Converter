Audio Converter

This app converts your audio files into the proper format for the Sony Clie’s built‑in player, and the resulting files must be placed in /PALM/Programs/MSAUDIO on your Memory Stick.

Drag & drop your audio files here or click to browse

Supported formats: WAV, MP3, FLAC


Tested on Sony Clié SJ33, NR70, NX70, TG50, TH55 - Github project
